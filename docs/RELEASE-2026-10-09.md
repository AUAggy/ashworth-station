### Timing limits

Captures used native ANGLE/Metal on an Apple M1 Max, not a mobile GPU. Adaptive resolution and simulation were disabled during timing to prevent a lower pixel ratio from hiding a slower pass.

Raw RAF medians varied from roughly 50–100 ms across serial captures, including repeated unchanged-reference captures. In the final capture, desktop median was 66.7 ms versus 83.4 ms; phone medians were 83.2/83.4 ms versus about 66.7 ms; tablet median was 83.3 ms versus 66.7 ms. An earlier release pass had phone medians of 99.9 ms. The p95 values stayed near 133 ms. These higher mobile-profile medians are recorded, not suppressed or called improvements.

CPU/driver render-submission medians were about 7–8 ms. A separate phone-landscape diagnostic that added `gl.finish()` measured CPU submission plus completion wait at 7.2→7.7 ms median and 9.7→9.5 ms p95. That diagnostic changes synchronization and is not pure GPU time or a device certification. The headless pacing results do not support a stable FPS-gain claim. Sustained performance acceptance belongs on the intended devices.
